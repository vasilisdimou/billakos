Photo Uploading

1. Visit this free image hosting website and upload your photo: postimages.org

2. Copy the direct link (the 2nd) and use it in your message.

You can use:

<div class="blog-post"> <center> <img src="IMAGE LINK" width="400" height="400"> </center> </div>

You can write whatever you want and then add an image. You can also add text after the image. If you want more images, just add more <img src="IMAGE LINK"> elements.
