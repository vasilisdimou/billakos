My Favorite BreyerFest 2026 Models (Raffle, OOAKs & Prizes)

Published on 8 July 2026

Hello! This has to be the 3rd post this week, which is crazy! I am getting ready for BreyerFest, and today I wanted to show you my favorite models from the auction, raffle and prize models.

I am going to start with the Open Model Horse Show Overall Champion prize named Svalbard. Since I saw this one, I have been in shock. I don't know why, but this seems like such a pretty model that I would love to own one day. If you look at it against a dark background, you can see how this color pops and looks amazing.

Next are the two Best Custom Contest prizes: Lingonberry for the winners and Gravlax for the runners-up. I think the choice of these 2 models is great. I mean, there are only 7 models to make....

__ _______________ __

One of my favorites is Lot #3. I love the long tail on Wixom. Moving forward, Lot #5 on the Valegro mold will definitely be one of the most expensive ones, as it is a popular mold and this bay roan looks amazing!

Lot #13 is probably in my top 3 favorites of the batch. Look how cute this pony is!!! The blue saddle pad and the saddle with the blue details on a buckskin pinto horse are exactly what I would love to have right now.

Next is Lot #18... How cool is this polo pony? I recently saw someone who had every model on this Polo Pony mold, and I was thinking of them... They will definitely want it, not only because they would want to complete the conga, but also because Breyer made it look phenomenal.

Before revealing my favorite, Lot #21 and #22 are going to be the expensive ones. Lot #21 is a sleipnir, a pattern never seen before by Breyer on the Nokota mold, which is very popular. Drumroll... LOT #20. You might have guessed it; this model is so pretty. The braids and the short tail give the feeling of a Spanish horse doing working equitation. I love the customized mold and the buckskin dapples it has. The combination is phenomenal. I know for sure that it is no coincidence that they put the best ones later in the auction. There is going to be so much tension at the end!!!

__ _______________ __

The raffle models this year are great, but I like Sunday's a bit more. Walking Through Lupines is the reason I eliminated Bobby Jo from my surprise model prediction list.

__ _______________ __

Last but not least, I wanted to mention one Silent Auction lot, which is a test Stablemate model on the Mini Othello mold named Jumbo. Basically, it says "Test Jumbo Horatio." My theory is that it is actually the Traditional model they used to advertise Horatio for the 2024 Deluxe Collector Club.

Breyer News Roundup & Updates

Published on 14 June 2026

Hello! I have been a bit inactive lately due to my final exams. Last week I had three exams, and it was quite a rollercoaster since I had so much material to study. Thankfully, my next one is on the 22nd, which means I finally have some time to work on a few of my projects. I also submitted my Special Run preferences and placed The Wanderer as my top choice, with the Scandi Surprise as my second favorite.

__ _______________ __

There have also been some exciting releases lately that I wanted to share! First of all, the latest Collector Club web special was released and, unfortunately, I was not selected to purchase it. I am talking about Buenos Aires, a very cool polo pony. I genuinely love polo ponies and would like to add as many as possible to my collection, so this one will definitely make it onto my ISO list at some point.

Moreover, Breyer held a warehouse sale, but this time they only offered BreyerFest models and related items. There were quite a few things I wanted, but I decided to save my money for BreyerFest instead. I was tempted by the Stablemates that were only $8, some pins, Franz, and the BreyerFest 2018 signing model, which was only $12! Right now, because of the FIFA World Cup, Breyer is also offering Torlinie for free with purchases of $50 or more. I think that's a great deal. My guess is that this model wasn't a fan favorite, which might explain why they still have enough stock to give them away as a promotion.

Breyer also did something they have never done before and revealed the Special Run variations ahead of BreyerFest. I have mixed feelings about this. On one hand, it's nice to see the variations and submit preference forms knowing exactly what is available. On the other hand, it takes away some of the excitement and surprise. I always enjoy seeing collectors discover new variations and sharing photos as soon as they find them on Friday.

There is still a possibility that additional variations exist and Breyer is keeping those as a surprise.

__ _______________ __

Carmen, the first Premier Club model, has finally arrived and there seem to be quite a few quality issues. Many collectors received models with flaws, missing or very faint dapples, and overall lower quality finishes than expected. I believe this is unacceptable for a Premier Club release, and I completely understand why so many people are disappointed. That being said, I have also seen some collectors who were very happy with their models, but they appear to be in the minority. From what I have seen online, at least two out of every three owners were not satisfied with the quality of their Carmen. It will be interesting to see whether Breyer addresses these concerns or if the quality varies significantly from batch to batch.

__ _______________ __

This is one of the two midyear releases, and I am in love! His name is Mr. Shining Gun, and I like him a lot. I think Breyer did an excellent job with the details, and I can't wait to see one in person.

Last but not least, this is the Open Model Horse Show Overall Champion prize named Svalbard. I really admire this model! The color is stunning, and it looks fantastic on this mold. I would absolutely love to add one to my collection someday, although I highly doubt that will happen.

Thank you for reading today's post! I have a few more posts planned before BreyerFest. One will feature my surprise model prediction list (last year I failed because I crossed Totilas off my list at the last minute after they used a glossy Salvino as a prize model, and I assumed they wouldn't use the mold again that year). The other post will probably focus on the auction OOAKs. I'm thinking about making a list with my estimated prices for each model and then seeing how close I get to the actual auction results :)

Medallions

Día de los Muertos

Published on 2 November 2024

Today is Día de los Muertos, or the Day of the Dead, a beautiful Mexican holiday that honors and remembers loved ones who have passed away. Last year, I was incredibly privileged to visit Mexico and experience this profound tradition firsthand. Día de los Muertos is celebrated with vibrant colors, elaborate altars (known as ofrendas), and a joyful remembrance of those who have passed. Families come together to create these ofrendas, which are decorated with photos, marigold flowers, candles, and favorite foods of their loved ones, inviting the spirits to visit and celebrate together. It’s a time to remember with joy rather than mourn with sadness, believing that the spirits of those we’ve lost are closer to us than ever on this special day. If you’ve seen the movie Coco, you may already have a good sense of what Día de los Muertos is all about.

Breyer 2025 Clubs so far!

Published on 19 October 2024

This post might be a bit early, and I’ll likely make another one when we get more details about the three specialty clubs. So far, Breyer has only announced the Collector Club.

This year looks very exciting just for being in the Collector Club. It appears that there will be another club event, plus a new store exclusively for members to purchase Collector Club merchandise and models. This is a new feature for this year, and we’ll have to wait and see what exactly this store will offer. Additionally, as always, they’ve revealed the first special run, which is Maddox. It is a stunning dapple gray on the Akhal-Teke mold. Some familiar benefits remain unchanged, such as the 10% discount, access to other clubs, workshops, exclusive blogs, and a copy of the JAH magazine (I still haven’t received mine).

As for the Deluxe Collector Club, I was right about the mini version of a popular mold, but I never expected there would be two Stablemates! This year, they’ve scaled down the Semi-Rearing Mustang, and members will receive Darius, a Bay Appaloosa, along with an extra Stablemate of the same mold in a random color. Here are the four possible colors:

My favorite is the Wedgewood (solid blue), but I wouldn’t mind getting any of the other options! Along with these Stablemates that ship for free (yay!), you also get a pin featuring Maddox.

Pricing

The pricing has increased again this year. I believe the club cost $25 in 2023, went up to $30 in 2024, and now it’s $35. The Deluxe option was $65 last year and is now $85 ($70 and $90 respectively for international memberships). If you want to join for the rest of 2024, it’s an additional $10 for each membership. In my opinion, the price increase for the Deluxe option makes sense because it includes an extra Stablemate but the increase in the regular membership fee without any additional Stablemate or pin seems a bit unfair.

I will probably join the Deluxe Collector Club again this year as I really like the Stablemates and will probably also like the pin!

Premier, Stablemates and Vintage club

Yesterday, someone shared a photo in a Facebook group that shows one release for the 2025 Premier Club and one for the Stablemates Club. I also noticed that the Vintage Club will have 1,200 members this year, which is 200 more than before.

I was right about the Premier Club too! I expected a new mule mold, and here it is! This makes joining the club very tempting, but I don’t think I will. The second horse in the image is a beautiful Arabian mare with blood marks. Initially, this model looked familiar, and I wasn’t sure if it was new or not. Then I saw a post from modelhorsecollectibility.blogspot.com, which explained that Hagen-Renaker allowed Breyer to scale down one of their models.

We’ll have to wait a few more days until Breyer shares more information about the clubs and their pricing. We’ll probably see every release from the Stablemate Club, at least one from the Vintage Club, and maybe another Premier Club mold without its coloring before the end of the year, as they did last time.

Have a great rest of your weekend!

2025 Clubs speculations

Published on 6 October 2024

Happy Sunday! It’s finally October, the time of year when Breyer typically announces next year’s clubs! I’ve been thinking about what they might do for 2025, and I wanted to share my speculations and ideas with you.

Collector Club

For the Collector’s Club I have no idea on what they could do. The only thing I can think of is that they might feature vintage molds/colorways. As for the exclusive Stablemate for the deluxe membership, my guess is that it will be a miniaturized version of a popular traditional mold but that is not unique as they have done that before.

Stablemates Club

About the Stablemates Club, I believe they’ll continue shrinking popular molds from different decades. There are typically six releases (plus one optional model), and I expect each to represent a horse from a particular era, ending with a brand-new, modern sculpture as they do every year. They could scale down some beloved molds from previous decades that collectors have been eager to see as Stablemates. Imagine a mini Ethereal!!

Premier Club

I have two ideas for the Premier Club next year. The first is that Breyer could introduce new sculptures people have been asking for, such as a Friesian, a donkey, or even a mule. The second idea seems a lot unlikely to happen but they could bring back sculptors from the past who created iconic molds. They could remake their original works in a modern style. While it’s more likely that Breyer would hire newer sculptors to do this, having the original artists return would make the molds extremely special and iconic.

Vintage Club

Next year’s Vintage Club is going to be interesting, especially with Breyer celebrating its 75th anniversary. Typically, each release in this club honors Breyer’s history, but I think for such a milestone, they’ll do something truly exciting and different. While I can’t pinpoint exactly what, I believe Breyer will surprise everyone with something unique to mark this important occasion.

All in all, I’m really interested to see what next year will bring. It’s definitely going to be fun! Have a nice rest of the day :)
