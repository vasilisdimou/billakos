Dear model horse collectors, I’ve come to the decision to shut down my website for now. Due to some personal issues, I don’t have the time or the courage to keep posting or maintaining the blog the way I’d like to. The Shows page will remain online (at least for now), since I’m still planning to host more shows in the future. The Catch the Horses mini game will also stay up as long as the website is active, and you can continue suggesting new models by emailing me at billakosmodelhorses@gmail.com. I might return in the future! This doesn’t mean I’m leaving the hobby. It’s simply a change to help me feel less overwhelmed and to give myself space to be more active in Facebook groups and on the Breyer forums. Thank you for understanding and for being part of my journey :)
